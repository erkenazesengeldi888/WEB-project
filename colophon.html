About this project

This website about Espresso Day was made by three students of group SE-2537 at Astana IT University as part of the Web Technologies course. In Assignment 1 we built the pages in HTML: semantic elements such as header, nav, main, section, article and footer, plus images, links, lists, tables and a form. In Assignment 2 we added styling with CSS: a shared base.css written together and one personal stylesheet for each student. The site does not use any CSS framework or JavaScript.

HTML example

The figure element is used to add a photo with a caption.

<figure>
    <img src="images/photo.jpg" alt="description">
    <figcaption>caption</figcaption>
</figure>

Project information

The website is about Espresso Day, a coffee shop chain in Astana, Kazakhstan. The pages present its menu, location, coffee and customer reviews, and the photographs on the site are our own.
