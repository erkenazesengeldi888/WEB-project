Coffee and Signature Drinks

EspressoDay at Syganak 24 is a coffee shop in Astana where visitors can choose traditional coffee drinks, signature beverages, fresh juices and smoothies. The menu combines familiar coffee options with unusual flavours and drinks. This page presents information about the drinks and the atmosphere of the coffee shop.

Some interesting positions include Şie Bumble, coffee with fresh juice, Kok Aspan lemonade and different types of raf coffee. New menu positions can be marked with JANA, which means "new" in Kazakh.

Guests can also choose Kazakh Raf, Salted Peanut Raf, Vanilla-Strawberry Raf and Latte Qurt. Visitors who do not want coffee can choose tea, fresh juices or smoothies. The take away format is also an important part of the coffee shop concept.

The Coffee Shop Concept

Manager: Ulugbek Saydullaev Faizullaevich

The manager of the EspressoDay coffee shop network is Ulugbek Saydullaev Faizullaevich. The main idea of the coffee shop is maximum accessibility for visitors.

The coffee shop uses a take away format. Special attention is paid to quick service. Drinks and hot food are intended to be served in approximately 5 minutes.

The design also includes elements of Kazakh ethnic style. This creates a connection between a modern coffee shop format and local cultural elements.

The concept can be described with the phrase maximum accessibility.

The manager's original statement is presented on the reviews page.
