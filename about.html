About the Coffee Shop

EspressoDay Kazakhstan is a popular coffee shop chain in Kazakhstan, known for its cozy atmosphere and convenient locations. The brand was born in Astana and has grown into a large network with more than 130 coffee shops across the country. EspressoDay positions itself as a “second home” where customers can relax, study, work, or meet friends. The coffee shop offers a variety of drinks, including classic coffee, refreshing lemonades, and seasonal beverages. Its menu also includes snacks, desserts, sandwiches, and other food options, making it suitable for both a quick coffee break and a longer visit. EspressoDay has a recognizable red-and-white visual style and actively communicates with its audience through social media. The brand also participates in different events and collaborations, helping it stay connected with its customers. With its combination of accessible locations, diverse menu, comfortable atmosphere, and recognizable branding, EspressoDay has become a familiar part of everyday coffee culture in Kazakhstan.

The Atmosphere

What makes Espresso Day special?

Its strong red-and-white visual identity makes the brand easy to recognize, while its large network of 130+ coffee shops across Kazakhstan makes it convenient for customers in different cities. The variety of drinks, snacks, and food also means there is something for different tastes. Most importantly, EspressoDay combines coffee culture with a sense of community, making it more than just a place to buy coffee.
