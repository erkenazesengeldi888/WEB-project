Visitor Impressions

This page presents information about visitors' impressions of EspressoDay at Syganak 24 in Astana. It includes photos of the interior and bar area, information about the coffee shop concept and a feedback form for visitors.

The page separates real information from personal opinions. The manager's statement is presented as a direct quotation, while the feedback form is provided for future visitor responses. This makes the page useful both as an information page and as an interactive part of the website.

Interior and Bar

The interior is an important part of the visitor experience. The bar is the main working area where drinks are prepared and orders are handled. Together, these spaces show how the coffee shop combines a practical service format with a comfortable environment.

Manager's Statement

Ulugbek Saydullaev Faizullaevich

The manager of the EspressoDay network describes the concept through accessibility and fast service. The take away format allows visitors to receive their orders quickly, while the interior includes elements of Kazakh ethnic style.

"Кофейня формата take away, быстрая отдача блюд как и напитков так и горячего в течении 5 минут без надобности ждать по 20+ минут, концептия кофейни это максимальная доступность в двух словах с оттенками казахского этнического стиля."

Ulugbek Saydullaev Faizullaevich, manager of the EspressoDay coffee shop network.

Statement date: September 13, 2026.

The quotation above is presented as the manager's statement rather than as an invented customer review.
