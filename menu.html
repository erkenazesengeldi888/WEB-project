Our Menu

Every drink has a photo and a price. Coffee comes in up to three sizes: pick a size and the price updates. Prices are in tenge (₸). Use the search to find a drink fast. Read more about the coffee shop or visit us in person.

Find a drink

Showing the full menu. Type a drink name, for example latte, matcha or mojito, and choose Search to narrow it down.

No drink matches your search. Choose Clear to see the whole menu again.

Ready to order?

Press Add under a drink, then change the quantity with − and +. Press Go to payment to see your order, the total, and choose pick-up or delivery.
